# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is for people who want better visibility into their diabetes data, and for the people who
help them. We want it to be welcoming to everyone regardless of experience, background, identity or health.

Expected:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and setup questions.
- Assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Never shame anyone's glucose numbers, treatment choices or devices.
- Protect privacy. Don't post anyone's health data or personal details, including your own unredacted data.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Medical advice presented as authoritative. Share experience; point people to their care team.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (@tivotyro) may edit or remove comments, lock threads, close issues or PRs,
and block contributors who violate this code. Report problems privately via a
[security advisory](https://github.com/mmxca/glookup-ha-integration/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
